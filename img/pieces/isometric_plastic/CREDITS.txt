Isometric Plastic Chess Pieces
================================

These chess piece images are derived from the "2D Chess Pack" by Screaming Brain Studios,
specifically the isometric "Plastic" material variant.

  Creator : Screaming Brain Studios
            https://opengameart.org/users/screaming-brain-studios
            https://www.patreon.com/ScreamingBrainStudios

  Source  : https://opengameart.org/content/2d-chess-pack

  License : Creative Commons Zero (CC0) – Public Domain
            http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The CC0 license means these assets are released into the public domain. No attribution
is legally required, but the creator has noted:
  "Credit is always appreciated, but never required :)"

The original 128×128 tile sheets were cropped, background-removed, and repacked into
horizontal PNG strips compatible with the scidCommunity chess interface.
