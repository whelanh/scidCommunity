Lichess puzzle database
=======================

  Source   : https://database.lichess.org/#puzzles
  File     : lichess_db_puzzle.csv
  Snapshot : 2026-11-11
  License  : Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)
             https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Lichess publishes its database exports under CC0: "Use them for research,
commercial purpose, publication, anything you like. You can download,
modify and redistribute them, without asking for permission."

The six PGN files in this directory are a selection of 6,000 puzzles from
that database, made with scripts/build_puzzle_sets.py. The positions and
solutions come from games played on lichess.org and are covered by the CC0
dedication above. Each puzzle keeps its Lichess PuzzleId and GameUrl so the
original can be inspected.

The selection itself -- which puzzles were chosen, the six rating bands, the
theme and phase quotas -- is part of scidCommunity and is released under the
same licence as the rest of scidCommunity (GNU General Public License,
version 2 or later; see ../COPYING).
